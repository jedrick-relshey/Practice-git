Healthy Habits

Students should maintain a healthy lifestyle to improve their focus and academic performance. They should eat nutritious meals, including fruits and vegestables, everyday. Dringking plenty of water is also important tostay hydrated. Student should get at least 8-10 hours of sleep every night. Regular exercise, such as walking or jogging, helps keep the body and mind healthy. They should take short breaks while studying to avoid stress and fatigue. It is also helpful to manage time wisely by following a study schedule. Limiting the use of social media while studying can improve concentration. Students should keep a positive attitude and ask for help when they feel overwhelmed. Finally, maintaining a healthy lifestyle helps students succeed in school and enjoy a balanced life.
